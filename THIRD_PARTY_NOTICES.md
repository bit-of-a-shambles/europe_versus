# Third-party design foundation

The publication layout is adapted from **Headline**, an official Ghost Foundation
news theme.

- Source: https://github.com/TryGhost/Headline
- Copyright: 2013–2026 Ghost Foundation
- License: MIT

The centered masthead, separate topic navigation, latest-story grid, restrained
type scale and responsive stacking model are derived from Headline. Europe in One
Chart uses its own Rails templates, content, chart system, brand and palette.

## Typefaces

Besley and Public Sans are used under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
Besley is maintained by the Besley Project authors; Public Sans is maintained
by the United States Web Design System. The production WOFF2 files are
self-hosted in `app/assets/fonts`; their license texts are stored beside them.
